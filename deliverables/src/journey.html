User Journey: from raw data to an approved allocation plan

One complete end-to-end path through the running MVP, using the synthetic demo scenario.

Actor Planner, simulated single local user, accountable for the weekly plan.
Goal Lower verified total cost within supply, capacity and lane limits, and keep the final say.
Scenario Synthetic demo: 2 suppliers, 3 warehouses, 6 zones, 20 SKUs, 606 pallets a week.

Steps 1 and 2: Data. One click loads the demo, or the planner uploads files. Counts and data checks appear before anything is calculated.
Steps 3 to 5: Results. Baseline 32,698 against optimised 31,316: saving 1,382 (4.2%), solver status and the independent check beside it. 15 decisions differ from the baseline.
Steps 6 and 7: Review and adjust. After approving the explanation, the planner fixes one decision: Zone 1, Cement standard, Warehouse B, exactly 8 pallets, with a reason.
Steps 8 and 9: Approve and audit. The run with that decision (31,404, which is 88 above the free optimum) is approved and locked; export unlocks; each step is logged.

Exception: invalid dataA negative rate or missing column is reported with file and row. The run is blocked until the file is corrected.
Exception: unusable baselineIf the baseline rule cannot serve all demand, the saving is shown as "n/a", never as a number. The optimised plan is still valid.
Exception: impossible decisionA planner decision that breaks capacity or lanes is rejected with an explanation, logged, and the current plan stays unchanged.
Exception: no AI or no networkThe deterministic summary is used. If AI text contains a number that was not calculated, it is discarded for the deterministic one.

What the journey does not cover: real ERP, WMS or TMS connections (files in and out only), login and roles (one simulated planner), measuring planner time saved or on-time delivery (needs real users).
