User journey: from loading data to an approved, exported plan

One complete path through the MVP, recorded on the running application with the synthetic demo scenario. Screens are real; figures are synthetic cost units.

Who
A planner in Company A's Swedish pilot region (simulated), who today allocates goods by habit, one leg at a time.
Goal
Decide which supplier and which existing warehouse should serve each customer zone at the lowest total cost, keep control of the result, and be able to show why.
Scenario
2 suppliers, 3 existing warehouses, 6 customer zones, 20 products, 606 pallets in one weekly run.
1Load and check→ 2Calculate→ 3Compare routes→ 4Review→ 5Decide→ 6Approve and export Steps 1 to 3 on this page, 4 to 6 on page 2
1

Load and check the data

PlannerPicks the synthetic demo, or uploads the 8 CSV files or one ZIP. SystemChecks every file before any calculation. Errors block the run, with file, row and message. ResultA valid scenario: 606 pallets of demand, 954 pallet-equivalents of weekly capacity.
2

Calculate and read the result

PlannerSelects Calculate plan. SystemBuilds the preferred-warehouse baseline, solves for the lowest cost, and re-checks both plans independently. Result31,316 against 32,698: a saving of 1,382 (4.2%), shown only because the baseline is feasible and verified.
3

See where goods move and why

PlannerReads the allocation diagram and the complete route cost ranking for a customer and product. SystemPrices each full path, supplier to warehouse to customer, with freight and handling at every leg. ResultEvidence for each choice, down to the cost of a single leg. Highlighting never changes the plan.

NEW AURA · Nordic Flow · Round 2

The planner stays in control: review, decide, approve

Steps 4 to 6 of the same journey. A human reads the explanation, can override the machine with a reason, and nothing leaves the system until the plan is approved.

1Load and check→ 2Calculate→ 3Compare routes→ 4Review→ 5Decide→ 6Approve and export
4

Review the explanation

PlannerGenerates an explanation and opens the supporting figures. SystemBuilds the text from calculated numbers. With the optional AI assistant, any figure not in those numbers causes the draft to be rejected. ResultA draft the planner approves, edits or restarts. AI never calculates.
5

Apply a human decision

PlannerFixes 8 pallets of Cement standard for Zone 1 to Warehouse B, with a required reason. SystemRe-optimises everything else around the decision as a new version and keeps the original. ResultPlan 2 costs 31,404, which is +88 cost units. The price of the decision is visible.
6

Approve, export and audit

PlannerApproves the explanation and the plan, then exports the ZIP. SystemLocks the plan, enables export only after approval, and records every step in a hash-linked audit trail. ResultA ZIP for the existing ERP, WMS and TMS. Nordic Flow executes nothing.

What-if. Close a warehouse on a copy of the data. If no plan can serve demand, the system says so and lists likely causes, never inventing a route.
Start a new version. Approved and rejected plans are locked. A new draft is calculated, and earlier versions stay in the history.
Optional extensions. Transfers between existing warehouses and direct supplier-to-customer lanes switch on only when extra input files are supplied. This journey uses neither.
All data is synthetic. The baseline is a rule that prefers one warehouse per zone, not Company A's actual plan, and the saving is not a forecast for Company A.
